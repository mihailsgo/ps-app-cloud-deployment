# 13.6 What is the format of the signed document, and how do the signature and seal appear?

- The result is a **PDF**.
- The **visual signature** (the handwritten signature from the tablet) is
  placed into the PDF's content by the signature service, so it is visible
  on the page in every viewer.
- When sealing is on, a **digital seal** is added: an electronic seal in
  the PDF, made either by the external e-sealing service or by the local
  stamping service ([10. Local e-sealing](10-local-e-sealing.md)).
- A PDF viewer that shows signatures (for example Adobe Acrobat Reader's
  signature panel) lists the signature and seal with their certificates.
  What is shown depends on the viewer.
